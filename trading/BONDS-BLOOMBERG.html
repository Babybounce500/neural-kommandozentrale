Key Fixed-Income Risk Formulas
ΔP (1st order) ≈ −D_mod × P × Δy
ΔP (2nd order) ≈ −D_mod × P × Δy + ½ × C × P × (Δy)²
DV01 = D_mod × Dirty Price × 0.0001 × (Notional/FaceValue)
BPV / PVBP = D_mod × Price / 10,000
Dollar Duration = D_mod × Price × Notional / 100
Convexity Adj. = ½ × Convexity × P × (Δy)²
Note: Positive convexity benefits LONG bond holders — price rises faster than it falls.
Macaulay Duration: Σ [t × PV(CF_t)] / Price
Modified Duration: Mac.Dur / (1 + YTM/freq)
Convexity: Σ [t(t+1) × PV(CF_t)] / [Price × (1+r)² × freq²]

G-Spread Calculator (Bloomberg YAS → G-Spd)

G-Spread = Bond YTM − Interpolated Government Benchmark yield (same tenor)

Z-Spread (Static Spread to Spot Curve)

Z-Spread: constant spread added to every point on the spot curve so DCF equals dirty price. More precise than G-spread.

Uses current Nelson-Siegel curve from ICVS tab as base spot rates.

OAS — Option-Adjusted Spread
OAS = Z-Spread − Embedded Option Value
Callable bond: OAS < Z-Spread (issuer's call option costs you spread)
Putable bond: OAS > Z-Spread (investor's put option is worth spread premium)
In Bloomberg OVME/YAS: OAS computed via binomial interest-rate tree (CRR or BDT model)
Rule: For option-free bonds → OAS ≈ Z-Spread ≈ G-Spread (approximately)
